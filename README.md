### Marketing Analytics SQL Project – NeoBanker

Welcome to my **Marketing Analytics Project** repository 🚀

NeoBanker is a fictional digital-only bank operating in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa. The company has grown rapidly through aggressive marketing spend on Meta, Google, TikTok, Twitter, and Apple Search

---

**NeoBanker offers:**

- Free debit card (interchange revenue)

- Instant transfers (transaction fees)

- Airtime & bill payments

- Savings accounts (interest spreads)

- Microloans (interest margin)

The Growth & Marketing team is spending heavily, but leadership wants to know if campaigns are efficient and sustainable.

---

### ❓ Problem Statement

**NeoBanker leadership is concerned about:**

- Acquisition efficiency — Are campaigns generating profitable users?

- Activation funnel — Why do many installs fail to complete KYC or fund accounts?

- Retention & churn — Are users active long enough to cover acquisition costs?

- Lifecycle campaigns — Do CRM nudges actually increase funding?

- Virality — Is the referral program truly driving growth?

---


### 🎯 Aim of the Analysis

**This project analyzes the marketing and user behavior to:**

- Track the funnel: Install → KYC → First Funding → Transactions

- Compute CAC, ROAS, Payback, CLV/CAC by campaign/channel

- Measure user retention & churn

- Evaluate CRM experiment incrementality (treatment vs control)

- Quantify referral program effectiveness (K-factor)
